Update your snapshot

Export your library from Playnite as CSV, then rebuild with your new export. Existing cover art and saved notes are kept.

./library/tools/update.sh --csv /path/to/playnite_library.csv

To refresh your Steam wishlist, replace library/steam-wishlist.json and run:

./library/tools/update-wishlist.sh

Reload the page after updating. Full update instructions
