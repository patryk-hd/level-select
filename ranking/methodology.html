How ratings work

Choose a source to see its reception separately. A score describes that source's opinion or review population. It does not measure your personal interest or prove that one game is better for everyone.

Available modes

Saved Steam ratings
The wishlist defaults to conservative approval from exact positive and negative counts. Its Wilson lower bound uses z = 1.96 and adds no popularity bonus. Raw approval percentage and review volume are separate sort options. Volume measures popularity, not quality. All languages and purchase types are included; Steam's off-topic filtering is enabled.
VGC Score
The VideoGamesCritic score is displayed unchanged, with attribution and a link to the game. Its components are not averaged into another score. Current mappings establish work-level context, not a score for a specific console release.
IGDB community / critics
Separate provider aggregates, displayed unchanged. These also remain work-level context; a platform in the provider's metadata does not establish the score's platform population.
Imported ratings
Playnite critic, community and personal fields remain separate per copy. Their original providers, counts and dates are unknown. Imported wishlist approval is an explicit alternative to saved provider data; rounded percentages produce an approximate Wilson result. Personal scores never alter public reception.
Legacy editorial order
The original catalog order is still available as a labeled reference while verified system reception is incomplete. Source modes do not fall back to this order or old maximum scores. Accepted challenges preserve the positions they were created with.

Scope, missing data and filters

A Steam product ID identifies a product. An imported title lookup does not verify that an owned copy is that product. Owned copies need reviewed provider mappings; multiple matching copies remain unranked until you choose a platform/store that identifies one copy. Detail inspection lets you examine copies separately.

VGC and IGDB modes order mapped records by their work-level value. They are not system rankings. The catalog's Top 10/30 per system filter is unavailable in these modes. Selecting a system or ownership filter narrows the records displayed without claiming that the provider rated those specific releases.

Unknown values stay unranked and are placed after rated records. There is no automatic fallback to an import, another provider, a later port or an old score. Zero is a real value when supplied by valid evidence. Unreleased wishlist games are excluded from reception rankings and genre cutoffs.

Wishlist and owned-copy positions come from their full imported cohorts; filters preserve those positions. Game Atlas positions are calculated within the filtered family/system/search selection and update as it changes. Legacy ranks are shown only for a single console. Title/date lists use list positions, not a combined console ranking. Equal values share a position; title and stable ID order ties. These positions are not global ranks of all games. “Top by genre” takes the highest rated eligible records from the selected source in the filtered genre; unrated and unreleased games are never removed by its cutoff.

Freshness and reproducibility

Pages read local saved snapshots. They do not fetch provider ratings in the browser. Refresh with npm run ratings:refresh, then reload. The initial mapping set covers Elden Ring; --wishlist opts in exact Steam IDs from your imported wishlist. VGC and IGDB additions need identity review.

Stale data and failed refreshes retain the last accepted evidence with a visible label. “Cache current” describes the last successful check, not when every underlying review was written. Game details show source links, scope, counts, observation time, method version, evidence snapshot and candidate-set identifier. A bookmarked mode uses the currently saved snapshot; it does not freeze future refreshes. Accepted challenges remain frozen separately.

Saved Steam reviews may not represent all players; Wilson uncertainty does not correct selection bias. We do not invent confidence intervals for aggregate means or rank-stability bands without supporting observations.

What comes next

Retro consensus needs verified release-specific evidence from independent sources, enough overlap and a connected comparison set. It is not enabled yet. Personalized play priority is separate from reception and will honor manual choices. Existing play history and accepted challenges are unchanged.
