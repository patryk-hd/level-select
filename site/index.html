macOS won’t open it?

Level Select isn’t notarized by Apple yet.

  1. Drag Level Select to Applications and open it once.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Next to the message about Level Select, choose Open Anyway.

Prefer to run it from source? It needs Node.js 22 or later: see the README.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no premium tier, no account. It’s open source under the MIT licence.

Does it change anything in Steam, GOG Galaxy or Playnite?

No. It only reads your games from them. It never buys, installs or posts anything.

Where do the scores come from?

IGDB (critics and players), Steam reviews, VideoGamesCritic and the review boxes on Wikipedia, each credited next to the score. Awards come from Wikidata.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes, without an account. At home, open On your phone in Level Select and scan the QR code: your phone browses your library over your Wi-Fi, and you can add it to your home screen. Away from home, Level Select keeps a copy of your library in your own iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Dropbox, and the phone app opens it. Nothing goes through a Level Select server.

What does the demo show?

Real games with public box art and scores, in a made-up library: which store each game is on and the play time are invented. Importing and updating work in the app you install.
